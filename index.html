    FAQ

    Do I need a smartwatch?

    No. A watch unlocks live heart-rate zones, but every plan works with just your phone.

    I'm a complete beginner. Is Pulse for me?

    Yes — beginner plans start with 20-minute sessions and build up gradually.

    Can I train at home without equipment?

    Absolutely. Choose "no equipment" and Pulse will only suggest bodyweight exercises.

    How do I cancel Premium?

    In two taps from your profile. You keep access until the end of the paid period.
